# Trademarks

## Marks are separate from open specifications

The FCL Standard Foundation publishes open specifications under CC BY 4.0 so that the **technical and documentary content** can be read, shared, and implemented. That copyright license does **not** include a trademark license.

Names, logos, trade dress, and certification marks associated with the Foundation and the FCL family of instruments remain subject to trademark and related laws and to Foundation policy.

## Publication and implementation do not authorize mark use

Publishing a draft, forking this repository, citing these documents, or building software that implements the data model does **not** authorize use of Foundation trademarks or certification marks.

## “FCL Certified” and related marks

Use of “FCL Certified” or any Foundation certification mark is permitted **only** under the separately published certification rules and any associated mark-use conditions. See:

- [specs/certification/FCL-CERTIFICATION-RULES-0.1.md](specs/certification/FCL-CERTIFICATION-RULES-0.1.md)
- [specs/registry-mark/FCL-REGISTRY-MARK-0.1.md](specs/registry-mark/FCL-REGISTRY-MARK-0.1.md)

## What a mark does not mean

Display or mention of an FCL-family mark or certification indicator does **not**, by itself, mean that:

- the Foundation owns the underlying copyrights, trademarks, publicity rights, or other rights in the subject work, world, catalog, or identity;
- a particular work is “official,” “canon,” or Foundation-approved creative content; or
- the Foundation endorses a vendor, product, platform, or commercial offering.
