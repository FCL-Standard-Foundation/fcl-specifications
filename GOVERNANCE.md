# Governance

## Steward

The **FCL Standard Foundation**, a West Virginia nonprofit organization, stewards the FCL family of specifications published in this repository.

## Drafts for comment

Documents here are primarily **drafts published for comment**. They are not, by publication alone, adopted formal standards of a government or accredited standards body.

## Maintenance

Today the Foundation operates with a **sole director** responsible for stewardship of these drafts. Processes for additional maintainers, technical directors, or broader review bodies may be published as the project grows.

This note intentionally leaves room for that growth. It does **not** invent a board, working-group charter, or voting procedure that does not yet exist.

## Scope of stewardship

Stewardship includes:

- publishing and versioning specification drafts;
- receiving comments and tracking open issues;
- keeping licensing and trademark notices accurate; and
- coordinating certification-rule publication separately from the open specification text where required.

Certification-mark authorization and registry operations, when offered, are governed by their own published rules—not by informal commit access alone.
